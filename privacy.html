Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the video game PROJECT_JUMP, its Discord integration, its map kit and related services (together, the "Service") collect, how it is used, and the choices you have. The Service is provided by the PROJECT_JUMP development team ("we", "us"). In short: we do not ask you to create an account, we do not sell your data, and we collect as little as the game needs to work.

1. Information we collect

From Steam

PROJECT_JUMP runs through Steam. When you play, the game receives from Steam your Steam ID, your Steam display name and avatar, your friends list, and information about lobbies you host or join. We use this to identify you in multiplayer, show who is in a lobby, run the scoreboard and leaderboards, and deliver Workshop content. We do not receive your email address, password, payment details or real name from Steam.

Gameplay data

The game records finish times, the route and map played, the settings used during the run, and a sampled path of your movement (a "replay"). Leaderboard entries are stored with your Steam display name and Steam ID so that they can be shown to you and to other players. Replays and settings are stored on your own computer. Where online leaderboards are offered, finish times and the identifying details above are sent to our servers.

Chat and lobbies

Messages you send in in-game chat are delivered to the other players in the same lobby through Steam's networking. We do not store chat history on our servers. The player hosting a lobby may see your Steam ID and connection information, as is normal for peer-to-peer play.

Player-made content

If you publish a map to the Steam Workshop, Steam associates it with your Steam account and shows your display name publicly. Maps you subscribe to are downloaded to your computer by Steam.

Crash reports and logs

If the game crashes, it may offer to send a crash report. A crash report contains technical information about your computer (operating system, graphics hardware, driver versions), the game's log for that session and a memory dump of the game process. It may incidentally include your Steam display name or Windows user name if they appear in file paths. Crash reports are only sent if you choose to send them. Log files stay on your computer unless you send them to us yourself.

Discord Rich Presence

If the Discord desktop app is running while you play, PROJECT_JUMP tells it what you are doing (for example the map and route you are on and how far along you are) so that it can be shown as your activity. This happens locally between the game and your Discord client. We do not receive any information from Discord, we do not access your Discord account, and no data is sent to us. Discord's handling of your activity is covered by Discord's own Privacy Policy. You can turn activity sharing off in Discord's settings at any time.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may log your IP address and browser details when they serve the page. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies on this website.

2. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, home address, payment information, precise location, voice, or the contents of files on your computer other than the game's own files described above. We do not use tracking or advertising technologies, and we do not build profiles of you for marketing.

3. How we use information

  • to run multiplayer, lobbies, chat and the scoreboard;
  • to show leaderboards, replays and your own run history;
  • to deliver Workshop maps and let mappers publish them;
  • to show your activity on Discord and Steam if you have that enabled;
  • to find and fix bugs and crashes;
  • to keep the game fair, including removing cheated results;
  • to comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share information only:

  • with other players, to the extent needed for multiplayer, chat and public leaderboards (your display name, finish times and in-lobby presence);
  • with Steam, Discord and our hosting providers, who process it under their own policies to provide their services;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of players or the Service.

5. Retention

Data stored on your own computer stays there until you delete it or uninstall the game. Leaderboard entries on our servers are kept for as long as the leaderboards exist, so that the history of a map remains meaningful. Crash reports are kept for as long as needed to investigate the problem, then deleted.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Discord activity: turn it off in Discord under Settings → Activity Privacy.
  • Steam profile and friends: managed through your Steam privacy settings.
  • Crash reports: choose not to send them when the crash reporter appears.
  • Local data: delete the game's data folder in your local application data directory to remove settings, replays and local leaderboards.
  • Leaderboard removal: ask us and we will remove your entries from any online leaderboards.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us as described below. We will need your Steam ID to find your data.

7. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Service, contact us and we will remove the information.

8. Security

We keep the data we hold to a minimum and protect it with reasonable technical measures. No online service can be completely secure, so please take care with what you share in public chat.

9. International transfers

Steam, Discord, GitHub and Google operate worldwide, so information handled by them may be processed outside your country. Any servers we operate may also be located outside your country. By using the Service you consent to this.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be announced in the game or on the PROJECT_JUMP Discord server.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about your data can be sent to us through the PROJECT_JUMP Discord server or through the contact details on the game's Steam store page.
