Patch Notes

What changed in each update, newest first.

Version 0.3.0

Latest

Version 0.3.0 is live. Please restart Steam if you don't see the update.

MovementRPGRunsLeaderboards & replaysSpectatingHUD & menusMultiplayerCustom mapsOther

Movement

  • Movement and collision went through a full review against the original game; lots of small fixes so it plays closer to it
  • Sprint has no time limit, lasts through the air, and ends on fire, jump, crouch, aiming or a second sprint press
  • Holding sprint through a jump brings sprint back mid-air (space and mouse-wheel jumps differ here, like the original)
  • Crouch can be held or toggled; if you can't stand up you stay crouched; the jump key stands you up from a crouch
  • Aiming slows walking like the original
  • Mouse-wheel jumps and other very short taps always register now
  • Landing and bounce detection is closer to the original, including how much speed a long fall costs
  • Sloped surfaces no longer hold you further away than they should
  • Invisible walls and ramps made with blocking volumes now collide exactly like normal level geometry
  • New ice surfaces in maps: no friction, and slopes slide you downhill

RPG

  • Pressing fire while sprinting now just ends the sprint; keep holding fire and it shoots once the launcher is up
  • New RPG switch option: the launcher is put away the moment it fires, for a clear view after a bounce
  • New RPG zoom option (off by default) with adjustable strength
  • A weapon put away mid-reload keeps reloading (this also closes the put-away reload skip)
  • RPG settings are now together under one RPG heading in Controls

Runs

  • Practice is fully separate from your run: rockets fired and moves made in practice no longer count toward it
  • Walking back into the start box without a save now resets the run, with a notice
  • Watching a replay mid-run pauses your run clock and no longer messes up your own run's recording
  • Watching a replay no longer gives you a free reload
  • Stepping back to an older save (hold LOAD + FIRE) now makes that save your current one
  • Fixed stepping back onto a start-box save resetting your load count

Leaderboards & replays

  • New leaderboard look: your run, your best with the PB difference, and your rank at the top, plus WATCH buttons
  • Routes are now chips you can click instead of arrows
  • LOW-RPG runs with the same rocket count in the same second are now decided by the milliseconds
  • Maps can have up to 32 routes with online leaderboards
  • Replays now pause on the last frame so you can scrub back or watch again
  • New replay viewer: timeline with saves, loads and rockets marked

Spectating

  • Pick who to watch from the scoreboard: hold Tab, right-click, then click a player (click yourself to go back)
  • See the watched player's keys light up, plus their PMOVE rate and speed
  • Free camera hides the HUD; the mouse wheel sets the fly speed
  • New card showing who is watching you, and who else is watching the same player
  • Your own launcher no longer shows while spectating
  • If the player you're following leaves, the camera moves to the next player instead of snapping back to you

HUD & menus

  • New HUD look to match the menus: notices with icons, vote card with countdown and YES/NO keys, new chat, restart prompt, name tags and FPS wheel
  • New default HUD layout (only applies if you never moved your HUD)
  • HUD editor shows every element's outline, name and size
  • Difficulty filter (ALL, 1-10) on SELECT MAP and when hosting
  • Type your own PMOVE rate (snaps to the closest rate the game can run)
  • Workshop lists maps by their in-game name

Multiplayer

  • A player losing connection no longer closes the host's lobby
  • Accepting an invite while already in a game now works
  • Chat spam limit to protect slower connections

Custom maps

  • Custom maps are checked more thoroughly before they load: a map can't touch your files, settings, the internet or the leaderboards
  • Map makers get new Blueprint nodes for launch pads, teleporters and low-gravity zones, plus more freedom for interactive maps
  • Replays downloaded from the leaderboards are checked before they load

Other

  • Developer console commands are no longer available to players
  • Fixed a hand-edited config breaking HUD scale or position
  • Unknown keys in the config no longer wipe a bind
  • Removed a leftover "Hello World" message on map start
